Summary of m0002/v0009 discussion (V0009: CFP for Mailing 0003)

The submeeting strongly supported V0009’s core move: forcing Mailing 0003 proposals to be concrete edits against `vyr-north-star.xml` using explicit diff markup, instead of abstract position papers.

Core substantive convergence:
- Participants agreed the CFP scope is well chosen: refine the north-star by proposing add/change/remove edits with explicit rationale and tradeoff analysis.
- The `<add>/<del>` excerpt format was seen as a good balance between human readability and machine-assisted review for mailing-style workflows.
- The group converged on a specific amendment bundle to reduce ambiguity and improve tooling readiness:
  - baseline references should be immutable (commit hash or immutable tag) rather than branch tips,
  - `<proposal-diff>` wrapper should be required (minimal required attributes: `source`, `base`),
  - diff excerpts should be explicitly treated as presentation markup (not required to remain well-formed XML after markup insertion),
  - conflict resolution guidance should be explicit for overlapping edits in meeting discussion,
  - requirement ID conventions should be clarified (semantic kebab-case, stable IDs, no reuse of retired IDs).
- There was additional alignment that optional enhancements (priority labels, visualization URLs) should remain optional guidance, not submission requirements.
- Several participants emphasized keeping structural reorganization out-of-scope unless it directly enables requirement-level changes, to keep M0003 focused.

Overall substantive outcome:
- The thread moved from “adopt as written” to “adopt with a lightweight, concrete amendment bundle” that preserves simplicity while making submissions more auditable and automatable.
- Consensus supported proceeding to publication of a revised/final CFP with the above guardrails.
