Summary of m0002/v0007 discussion (V0007: Process Refinements Consolidation)

The submeeting broadly supported V0007 as a needed process-constitution document, with discussion focused on preventing process ambiguity, gatekeeping drift, and backlog stagnation as participation scales.

Core substantive convergence:
- Participants agreed V0007’s baseline structure is strong: explicit roles, editorial gate, rough-consensus with deferred fallback, revision boundaries, and evaluation rubric (especially tradeoff-honesty and validation-feasibility).
- The largest concern was legitimacy and consistency of the editorial gate. Consensus fix: publish a transparent Editor Checklist as a companion artifact rather than keep "quality" implicit.
- The deferred-item "parking lot" was identified as a liveness risk unless lifecycle-managed. Consensus fix: Sponsorship-or-Sunset, with explicit state transitions and archive behavior instead of indefinite accumulation.
- Provenance language was tightened in discussion from soft/optional wording to default-required (with "unknown" only for genuinely opaque cases).
- The pseudo-code requirement was scoped to design/goal/feature proposals, avoiding misapplication to process/meta/legal papers.
- Editor governance/COI was recognized as a gap; consensus supported adding minimal guidance on appointment/rotation and recusal on own papers.
- Multiple participants converged on explicit lifecycle semantics for deferred items (Deferred/Active/Archived transitions recorded in notes) to preserve auditability.
- A versioned checklist identifier model (EC####) was endorsed so checklist evolution remains traceable without repeatedly amending the base process paper.
- Structured rejection rationale formatting was supported so rejection data can be analyzed and fed back into CFP guidance and submission templates.
- A "sponsor next-step" field was generally supported as encouraged (non-mandatory), balancing momentum with flexibility.

Overall substantive outcome:
- The thread moved from "adopt process paper as-is" to "adopt with concrete governance and auditability hardening," centered on V0007R1 plus companion checklist EC0001.
- Discussion converged on lightweight but explicit control mechanisms rather than heavy governance: transparent checklist, lifecycle states, recusal norms, and structured rationale metadata.
