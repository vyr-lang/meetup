Summary of m0002/v0004 discussion (V0004: Tooling-First Design Goals for Vyr)

The core substantive shift in this submeeting was from KPI-driven tooling goals to durable language/toolchain invariants.

Early feedback agreed with the tooling-first direction but argued the original paper mixed three different layers:
- language-spec invariants,
- reference toolchain implementation targets,
- product/UX KPIs (acceptance/satisfaction/latency numbers).

Participants argued this made conformance brittle and potentially gameable. The consensus recommendation was to separate:
- spec-level, long-lived, conformance-testable invariants; and
- aspirational benchmark targets in appendices/non-normative sections.

Major discussion outcomes:
- AI-specific “hook” framing was de-emphasized in favor of syntactic regularity, locality of reference, and predictable semantics that naturally help both human and AI tools.
- A versioned, serialized Standard Object Model (SOM) for semantic graph/tool interoperability became a central requirement.
- Error recovery and partial-program analysis were elevated as core requirements, not optional ergonomics.
- The thread introduced and converged on “grammar stratification”: parsing/recovery must not depend on type/name resolution.
- The thread also hardened “bounded unknown propagation”: unknown/holes should remain locally useful without collapsing unrelated analysis.
- Non-goal language excluding solo-developer usage was softened to preserve lightweight/single-file experimentation.
- Performance-profiling integration (original Goal 8) was considered architecturally risky and deferred to a separate paper.
- Framing was clarified: tooling is a means to cognitive clarity, correctness, and productivity, not an end in itself.

The revised draft posted in-thread was broadly endorsed as successfully incorporating these changes, with only minor remaining editorial polish requested:
- replace/update placeholder sections with concrete new examples,
- explicitly clarify the strict-vs-guiding distinction between grammar stratification and syntactic regularity/locality.

Overall substantive outcome:
- The submeeting transformed V0004 from a metrics-heavy proposal into a stronger architectural document centered on enforceable invariants and test strategies (editor-trace suites, metamorphic refactor tests, SOM compatibility tests).
- Consensus judged the revised direction suitable for broader M0002 circulation after minor editorial cleanup.
