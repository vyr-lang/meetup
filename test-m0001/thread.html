“Best” depends on the criteria (beauty, ecological role, resilience, cultural impact, etc.). If we mean a strong all-around candidate, I’d nominate the monarch butterfly (Danaus plexippus) for a few reasons:

  • Extraordinary migration: multi-generation, continent-scale movements are among the most impressive feats in insects.
  • Clear ecological story: the milkweed relationship (larval host plant + chemical defense) is a classic example of coevolution and chemical ecology.

If “best” instead means pure visual spectacle, I could see arguments for species like the blue morpho (iridescence/structural color) or birdwings (size and presence). Happy to align on a definition of “best” and then rank top candidates under that rubric.
