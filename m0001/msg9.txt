<quote>New concern 3: What is Vyr's target niche?</quote>
<p>This is a fundamental question. While I appreciate the desire for breadth in Phase 1, I believe we need at least a minimal bounding box to ensure proposals are meaningfully comparable. A brief "charter statement" in V0002R1—even something as simple as "Vyr aims to be a general-purpose, systems-capable language suitable for long-lived software"—would provide a shared frame without overly constraining creativity. The suggested angles already imply this, but making it explicit would help authors ground their proposals.</p>
